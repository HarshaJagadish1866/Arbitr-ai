FREELANCE SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

This Freelance Software Development Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
January 15, 2025 between TechStartup Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices at
123 Silicon Valley Road, San Francisco, CA 94102 ("Client"), and the independent
contractor engaged to perform services hereunder ("Contractor").

---

1. SERVICES

The Contractor agrees to perform software development services as requested by the
Client from time to time (the "Services"). The Client shall have the sole discretion
to determine the scope, priority, and timeline of all Services. The Contractor
shall be available during Client's business hours (9am-6pm PST) and shall respond
to communications within 2 hours.

---

2. PAYMENT

The Client agrees to pay the Contractor at the rate of $85 per hour for Services
rendered. Payment shall be made within 60 days of receipt of invoice. The Client
reserves the right to dispute any invoice and withhold payment for any disputed
amounts indefinitely until the dispute is resolved to the Client's sole satisfaction.

The Client may, at its sole discretion, adjust the hourly rate upon 30 days notice
to the Contractor. The Contractor's continued performance of Services after the
effective date of any rate adjustment shall constitute acceptance of the new rate.

---

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Contractor agrees that all work product, inventions, discoveries, improvements,
and developments created, conceived, or first reduced to practice by the Contractor
during or after the term of this Agreement, whether or not during working hours,
and whether or not using the Client's resources, shall be the exclusive property of
the Client. The Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Client all right, title,
and interest in and to all such work product and intellectual property. This includes
any pre-existing tools, frameworks, libraries, or code that the Contractor incorporates
into any deliverable.

The Contractor acknowledges that the work product constitutes "works made for hire"
under applicable copyright law.

---

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Contractor agrees to maintain in strict confidence all information relating to
the Client's business, technology, customers, and operations ("Confidential Information").
This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of ten (10) years.
The Contractor shall not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than
performing the Services. The Client's obligations regarding any information shared
by the Contractor are addressed solely by the Client's standard privacy policy,
which may be updated at any time without notice.

---

5. INDEMNIFICATION

The Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Client, its affiliates,
subsidiaries, officers, directors, employees, agents, successors, and assigns from
and against any and all claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including
attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to: (a) the Contractor's performance of
Services under this Agreement; (b) any breach or alleged breach of any representation,
warranty, or obligation of the Contractor; (c) any claim that the Services or any
deliverable infringes any third-party intellectual property right; or (d) any act
or omission of the Contractor or any subcontractor engaged by the Contractor.

The Client's liability to the Contractor under this Agreement shall not exceed $500.

---

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

IN NO EVENT SHALL THE CLIENT BE LIABLE TO THE CONTRACTOR FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL,
SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR DAMAGES FOR LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE,
DATA, BUSINESS, OR GOODWILL, EVEN IF THE CLIENT HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY
OF SUCH DAMAGES. THE CONTRACTOR SHALL REMAIN FULLY LIABLE FOR ALL SUCH DAMAGES.

---

7. TERMINATION

The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason,
immediately upon written notice to the Contractor. Upon termination, the Client
shall pay only for Services where all deliverables for that milestone have been
fully completed and accepted by the Client. No payment shall be made for partially
completed work, work in progress, or any costs incurred by the Contractor in
anticipation of future work.

The Contractor may only terminate this Agreement upon 90 days written notice to the
Client, and only if the Client has materially breached the Agreement and failed to
cure such breach within 60 days of notice.

---

8. NON-COMPETE

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months after
termination, the Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in, be employed
by, consult for, or have any interest in any business that is competitive with the
Client's business anywhere in the world. The Contractor further agrees not to solicit
or hire any of the Client's employees or contractors.

---

9. ARBITRATION

Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration
in accordance with the rules of the Client's legal department. The Contractor waives
any right to a jury trial. All arbitration proceedings shall be conducted at the
Client's principal place of business in San Francisco, California. The Contractor
shall bear all costs of arbitration unless the arbitrator determines the Contractor's
claim was not frivolous.

---

10. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without
regard to its conflict of laws provisions. The Contractor consents to the exclusive
jurisdiction of the courts of Delaware for any disputes not subject to arbitration.

---

11. ENTIRE AGREEMENT; MODIFICATION

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. The Client
reserves the right to modify any terms of this Agreement upon 24 hours written
notice to the Contractor. The Contractor's continued performance of Services after
such notice constitutes acceptance of the modified terms.
