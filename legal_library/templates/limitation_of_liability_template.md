## Template: Mutual Limitation of Liability with Standard Carve-outs

> **Section [X]: Limitation of Liability**
>
> **[X].1 Mutual Liability Cap.** EXCEPT AS SET FORTH IN SECTION [X].3,
> EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY TO THE OTHER PARTY ARISING OUT OF
> OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE),
> STRICT LIABILITY, OR OTHERWISE, SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR
> PAYABLE BY [CLIENT] TO [CONTRACTOR] IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY
> PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO SUCH LIABILITY.
>
> **[X].2 Mutual Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** EXCEPT AS SET FORTH IN
> SECTION [X].3, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT,
> INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, PUNITIVE, OR EXEMPLARY DAMAGES, INCLUDING
> ANY LOSS OF REVENUE, PROFITS, GOODWILL, OR BUSINESS, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE
> POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES AND REGARDLESS OF THE FORM OF ACTION.
>
> **[X].3 Exclusions from Limitation.** The limitations and exclusions in
> Sections [X].1 and [X].2 shall NOT apply to:
> (a) A Party's liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence;
> (b) A Party's liability for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;
> (c) A Party's liability for wilful misconduct or gross negligence;
> (d) Either Party's obligation to indemnify the other for third-party IP
>     infringement claims;
> (e) Either Party's breach of its confidentiality obligations under this
>     Agreement.
>
> **[X].4 Essential Basis.** The Parties acknowledge that the limitations of
> liability in this Section reflect a reasonable allocation of risk and are an
> essential element of the basis of the bargain between the Parties. Without
> these limitations, the fees charged under this Agreement would be significantly
> different.

## Why This Template is Protective

1. **Truly Mutual:** Both the contractor AND the client are subject to the same
   cap. Single-party caps (protecting only the client) are unfair.

2. **Carve-outs for Serious Wrongs:** The cap does NOT apply to fraud,
   gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or death/injury — these remain
   fully actionable regardless of cap.

3. **IP and Confidentiality Carve-outs:** These high-stakes areas remain
   outside the cap, protecting both parties from potentially catastrophic
   uncompensated harm.

4. **Mutual Consequential Damages Exclusion:** Both parties lose the ability
   to claim consequential damages — a fair trade that reduces litigation risk
   for both parties equally.

5. **"Essential Basis" Statement:** This recital helps enforce the clause if
   challenged — it shows both parties understood and accepted the limitation.
