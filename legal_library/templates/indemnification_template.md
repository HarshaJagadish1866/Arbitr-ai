## Template: Mutual, Fault-Based Indemnification

> **Section [X]: Indemnification**
>
> **[X].1 Mutual Indemnification.** Each Party ("Indemnifying Party") shall
> defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other Party and its officers,
> directors, employees, and agents ("Indemnified Party") from and against any
> third-party claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable
> attorneys' fees) ("Claims") to the extent such Claims arise directly out of
> or result from: (a) the Indemnifying Party's gross negligence or wilful
> misconduct; or (b) the Indemnifying Party's material breach of this Agreement.
>
> **[X].2 Exclusions.** No Party shall be obligated to indemnify the other Party
> for any Claims to the extent such Claims arise from: (a) the Indemnified
> Party's own negligence or misconduct; (b) the Indemnified Party's breach of
> this Agreement; or (c) actions taken by the Indemnified Party outside the scope
> of this Agreement.
>
> **[X].3 Indemnification Procedure.** The Indemnified Party shall: (a) promptly
> notify the Indemnifying Party in writing of any Claim for which indemnification
> is sought; (b) give the Indemnifying Party sole control over the defence and
> settlement of such Claim (provided that no settlement shall be made without
> the Indemnified Party's prior written consent if it imposes liability or
> obligation on the Indemnified Party); and (c) provide reasonable cooperation
> in the defence of such Claim at the Indemnifying Party's expense.
>
> **[X].4 Liability Cap.** Each Party's total indemnification obligation under
> this Section shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this
> Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the Claim.

---

## Why This Template is Protective

1. **Mutual:** Both parties are subject to the same indemnification obligation
   — neither party has a unilateral burden.

2. **Fault-Based:** Indemnification is triggered only by "gross negligence or
   wilful misconduct" or "material breach" — not mere performance failures
   or situations beyond the contractor's control.

3. **Explicit Exclusion:** Section [X].2 explicitly states that a party is NOT
   liable for the other party's own negligence — preventing the most common
   abuse of indemnification clauses.

4. **Proper Procedure:** The notice and control provisions ensure the
   Indemnifying Party can manage their own defence cost-effectively.

5. **Capped:** The liability cap in [X].4 limits total exposure to a
   reasonable amount tied to the contract value.
