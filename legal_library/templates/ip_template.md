## Template: Balanced IP Clause with Pre-existing IP Carve-out

> **Section [X]: Intellectual Property**
>
> **[X].1 Ownership of Deliverables.** Subject to full payment of all fees
> due under this Agreement, [Contractor] hereby assigns to [Client] all right,
> title, and interest in and to the deliverables specifically identified in
> Schedule A of this Agreement ("Deliverables"), including all intellectual
> property rights therein.
>
> **[X].2 Pre-existing Intellectual Property.** Notwithstanding Section [X].1,
> [Contractor] retains all right, title, and interest in: (a) any intellectual
> property owned by [Contractor] prior to the commencement of this Agreement
> ("Pre-existing IP"), including but not limited to the items listed in Schedule B;
> (b) any general methodologies, tools, frameworks, libraries, know-how, and
> processes used by [Contractor] in performing the Services; and (c) any
> developments independently created by [Contractor] without use of [Client]'s
> Confidential Information.
>
> **[X].3 License to Background IP.** To the extent any Pre-existing IP is
> incorporated into or necessary for the use of the Deliverables, [Contractor]
> hereby grants [Client] a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to
> use such Pre-existing IP solely to the extent necessary to use the Deliverables
> for [Client]'s internal business purposes.
>
> **[X].4 Condition Precedent.** The assignment in Section [X].1 is conditional
> upon [Client]'s full and timely payment of all fees due under this Agreement.
> Until such payment is made in full, [Client] receives only a non-exclusive
> license to use the Deliverables for internal purposes.
>
> **[X].5 No Other IP Rights.** Except as expressly set forth in this Section,
> no intellectual property rights are transferred or licensed from either Party
> to the other.

## Why This Template is Protective

1. **Assignment Limited to Deliverables:** Only specifically listed work product
   is assigned — not "any IP created during the term."

2. **Explicit Pre-existing IP Retention:** The contractor clearly retains tools,
   frameworks, methodologies, and anything they built before this contract.

3. **Payment-Conditioned Assignment:** IP only transfers after full payment —
   preventing the client from receiving IP and then refusing to pay.

4. **License (Not Assignment) for Background IP:** The client gets a practical
   right to use the pre-existing IP in the deliverable without owning it.

5. **No IP Beyond Scope:** The "no other IP rights" clause prevents creative
   interpretation to claim broader rights.
